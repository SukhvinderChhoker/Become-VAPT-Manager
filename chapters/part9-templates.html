📑 Why Documents Matter

Every VAPT engagement is built on documents. They are your legal shield, your sales tool, and your brand. Never start testing without signed paperwork.

⚠ LEGAL FIRST: In most jurisdictions, any unauthorized network or system probing is a criminal offense. RoE + LoA + SOW are your defense.
